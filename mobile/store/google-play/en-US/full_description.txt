Drop your discs into a board of 7 columns and 6 rows. Line up four in a row, across, down or diagonally, to win. The rules take a minute to learn; winning takes a little more.

■ No ads
No ads to interrupt you and no in-app purchases. Just open the app and play.

■ Take on the Super AI
The Super AI is tough and never goes easy on you. You always move first. Can you find a way to beat it?

■ Play with friends
Create a private room and send the room code or invite link to a friend. Friends without the app can join too: the invite link opens in any browser. After each game you can play again, and the first move alternates.

■ Match with players online
Tap once to look for an opponent online. When a player is found, the game starts and the series score is kept for you.

■ Clean and simple
• No sign-up, no login: you get a nickname right away and can change it anytime
• Games against the Super AI work without an internet connection
• English, Traditional Chinese and Thai
